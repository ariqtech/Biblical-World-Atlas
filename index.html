About this Atlas

This free, noncommercial atlas is an early working foundation. Source records, complete public-domain texts, original-language research, maps, genealogy and offline content packages will be reviewed and expanded. Readers can study and save private notes; only the project owner publishes shared research.

Research standard: distinguish scripture from commentary, manuscript evidence from conjecture, and reproducible geography from unverified proposals. Respect source and translation licenses.
