The Antarctic “Ice Wall” Claim

This research page explains what the alleged ice-wall theory claims and distinguishes real Antarctic ice shelves and cliffs from unsupported interpretations of Earth's geography.

Encircling wall claim: unsupportedAntarctic coast and ice fronts: documentedScriptural connection: thematic, not direct

What is being proposed?

Some modern flat-Earth accounts assert that Antarctica forms a continuous wall of ice around the outer edge of a physically flat Earth, sometimes accompanied by claims about undiscovered lands beyond it. These claims are not supported by reliable geographical observations, navigational measurements or satellite mapping. The scientific description is a continent surrounding the South Pole, bordered by the Southern Ocean.

Scientific observation

Ice shelves and steep ice fronts

Ice flowing from the interior can extend over ocean water, forming shelves with abrupt edges. These exist in Antarctica but do not form an Earth-encircling wall.

Mapped geography

Satellite and map evidence

The British Antarctic Survey, USGS and NASA publish extensive satellite and cartographic records of the continental landmass, ice shelves and surrounding ocean.

Primary treaty text

What the Antarctic Treaty says

The Antarctic Treaty of 1959 promotes peaceful uses, scientific cooperation and specified inspection rights. Its text does not document a world-encircling ice barrier or hidden territory beyond one.

Historical interpretation

Speculative southern-land maps

Some historical maps portray a hypothetical Terra Australis. A cartographer's proposed continent does not prove the existence of a modern ice wall or undiscovered land beyond Antarctica.

Biblical thematic references

Are there Bible passages about Antarctica?

No known biblical passage explicitly identifies Antarctica or a continuous Antarctic ice wall. References to ice, frost, creation, and seas below are thematic connections only. The claims cannot be verified simply by citing these passages.
