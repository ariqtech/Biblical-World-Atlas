How the References Are Classified

Explicit quotation

A Gospel writer quotes an earlier biblical passage or expressly references its wording.

Textual application

A Gospel writer uses an earlier passage in a new narrative setting; the original context still matters.

Composite quotation

Wording joins more than one earlier scriptural source. Compare each source independently.

Allusion or comparison

An identifiable echo or interpretive parallel, not necessarily a quotation or proof of a predicted event.

Important: A literary relationship can be documented without independently proving a historical or theological interpretation. The Atlas distinguishes biblical text, ancient context, and Matthew Henry's later Christian commentary.
