How to use this section

Scripture First · Commentary as a Study Aid

The Gospels are biblical texts; Matthew Henry's writing is later Christian commentary, not part of the biblical text. Each chapter provides two separate links: one for the King James Version reading and another for Henry's commentary.

All external readings open in a new browser tab where supported. Return to the Biblical World Atlas tab when finished to continue your study. The atlas stores only navigation links—not the complete text of the commentary.
