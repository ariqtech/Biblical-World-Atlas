Source, reading and copyright notes

These are external reading links, not embedded copies of entire books. A source that shows an excerpt or “See inside” panel is labeled as a preview; this does not guarantee that every chapter is available. Individual sources may require subscriptions, purchases, or library access. The Atlas does not bypass paywalls or copy books onto GitHub.

Some publisher previews may not open in all browsers or may require cookies or sign-in. If a direct preview fails, try the linked publisher/platform page for the same title, or select a clearly identified free commentary alternative.

Links open in a separate tab where the browser allows it. After reading, return to your original Biblical World Atlas tab to continue your research. The website cannot control another provider's reader, its selection/copy permissions, or its page content.
