Original archive

Historical maps and manuscripts

View an institutional catalog record, dated scan or digitized original. An old map shows what was drawn or believed in its time; it is not necessarily modern geographic proof.

Source data

Satellite images and scientific maps

Open scientific map viewers, projection information, and underlying observations. Large files stay at the source until an appropriately licensed research packet is released.

Reader

Scripture and ancient texts

Open the relevant edition at a reading website, respecting the text's copyright, language, manuscript tradition, and canonical status.

Scientific reference

Plants, soils and minerals

Read botanical or geological identification resources. A modern scientific entry does not automatically establish a disputed biblical word identification.
