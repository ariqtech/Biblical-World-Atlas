How to use this nature encyclopedia

Documented in Scripture means the term appears in the referenced passage; it does not prove that a modern plant or mineral species has been identified. Where a modern scientific identity is suggested, the record distinguishes a common identification from a historically uncertain or disputed one.

Science references lead to botanical and geological institutions for investigating modern taxa, rocks, soil and minerals. These do not independently verify the identity of an ancient Hebrew or Greek word.
