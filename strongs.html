Source and Interpretation Notes

Historical source: James Strong’s Exhaustive Concordance (1890), a historical public-domain reference. Its entries are not a substitute for contextual translation, Hebrew/Greek grammar or modern linguistic scholarship.

Digital delivery: this atlas uses BibleEngine’s publicly accessible, CORS-enabled static KJV, Strong’s lexicon and verse index files, fetched by your browser only when needed. No paid service, account or API key is required. Internet access is necessary for this remote dictionary data; full offline word-data packets remain a separate future option.

Important: a Strong’s number groups lexical forms for study, but a word can have different senses depending on its context. KJV tagging reflects a historical English translation and may differ from another Bible edition or critical Greek/Hebrew text. Occurrence counts are the source's indexed KJV results.
