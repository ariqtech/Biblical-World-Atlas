Research integrity

Historical maps are documents, not modern proofs

These maps reflect the knowledge and biblical interpretations of their publishers in 1888. Place names, proposed journeys, territorial outlines and ancient timelines may differ from newer archaeological and geographical research. They are not evidence that every illustrated route or boundary is certain.

Map provenance: American Bible Society, 1888; digitized copies displayed by GodWeb's public-domain Bible Atlas collection. Maps are reproduced here via the original host, which may require internet access. We attribute the publisher, year and digitizing source for each item.
