Reference & Usage Notes

Scripture: King James Version (KJV) passages are read through Bible Gateway. Other translations are available through the Bible Translation Library.

Commentary: Matthew Henry's Commentary on the Whole Bible is a historical Christian interpretation. Chapter links use the original reading site's book and chapter structure. A commentary is not Scripture and should not be treated as independent historical proof. Availability of individual chapters depends on the source site.

Storage and access: Only book names, chapter counts, and generated external links are used here. No bulk copyrighted commentary content or external full-text Bible copies are downloaded or saved to the Atlas repository. External readers need an internet connection. You can generally copy selectable text, but external sites may set their own access or copying restrictions.
